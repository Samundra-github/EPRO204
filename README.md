## 2. Selected Contexts & Required Content

### Context 1: Cultural and Diversity
*Topic: Supporting Immigrant and Refugee Families in Australian Early Childhood Education*
- **Section A (Understanding the Context):** ABS 2021 Census diversity statistics, AEDC LBOTE data, and Home Affairs humanitarian figures. Applied sociological theories: **Bronfenbrenner’s Bioecological Systems Theory**, **Bourdieu’s Cultural Capital Theory**, and **Social Constructionism**.
- **Section B (Impact on Children and Families):** Multilingual development, translanguaging, receptive silent period, pre-verbal trauma hyperarousal, and family settlement/CCS navigation.
- **Section C (Social Policy and Australian Responses):** National frameworks (EYLF V2.0, NQS QA1 & QA6, Inclusion Support Program) and South Australian policies (SA Preschool Bilingual Support Program, SA Multicultural Charter 2023).
- **Section D (Strategies for Practice, Birth–5):**
  1. *Translanguaging, Multimodal Visual Scaffolding, and Home-Language Story Baskets*
  2. *Trauma-Informed Primary Caregiving and Predictable Sensory Sanctuary Spaces*
- **Section E (Community and Professional Partnerships):**
  1. *Australian Migrant Resource Centre (AMRC – South Australia)*
  2. *STTARS (Survivors of Torture and Trauma Assistance and Rehabilitation Service, Adelaide)*
- **Section F (Resources for Educators and Children):**
  - Programs/Websites: *fka Children’s Services (fkaCS)* & *Raising Children Network Translated Resources Portal*
  - Children’s Storybooks (birth–5): *Whoever You Are* (Mem Fox) & *My Two Blankets* (Irena Kobald & Freya Blackwood)
  - Videos/Podcasts: *ABC Kids listen: Lullabies* & *Play School: Acknowledgement of Country*

### Context 2: Crisis and Emergency
*Topic: Supporting Children and Families Affected by Natural Disasters and Climate-Related Emergencies in Australia*
- **Section A (Understanding the Context):** Australian natural disaster data (2019–20 Black Summer bushfires, 2022–23 SA River Murray Floods, 1.4M children exposed annually). Applied sociological theories: **Bronfenbrenner in Disasters**, **Social Capital Theory (Bonding, Bridging, Linking)**, and **Socio-Ecological Resilience Theory ("Ordinary Magic")**.
- **Section B (Impact on Children and Families):** Environmental sensitivity (smoke, heat), neurodevelopmental distress, somatic regression, delayed learning impacts (*Beyond Bushfires* longitudinal research), and caregiver stress.
- **Section C (Social Policy and Australian Responses):** National mandates (Regulation 97 emergency rehearsals & NQS QA2, National Disaster Mental Health Framework, CCS Emergency Provisions) and South Australian policy (SA High Bushfire Risk Register, catastrophic day closures, Recovery SA).
- **Section D (Strategies for Practice, Birth–5):**
  1. *Therapeutic Story-Sharing and Child-Led Restorative Play Provocations*
  2. *Embodied Somatic Co-Regulation and Playful Emergency Readiness*
- **Section E (Community and Professional Partnerships):**
  1. *Emerging Minds* (Adelaide, SA)
  2. *Australian Red Cross Emergency Services* (SA & National)
- **Section F (Resources for Educators and Children):**
  - Programs/Websites: *Birdie’s Tree Early Learning Program* (QCPIMH) & *Emerging Minds Community Trauma Toolkit*
  - Children’s Storybooks (birth–5): *Birdie and the Fire* (Andrea Murray) & *Flood* (Jackie French)
  - Videos/Podcasts: *Birdie and the Fire Animated Story* (Children’s Health QLD) & *The Impact of Natural Disasters on Babies and Young Children* (Emerging Minds)
